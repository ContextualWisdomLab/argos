## 2023-10-04 - Prevent CSV Formula Injection
**Vulnerability:** Found a CSV Injection (Spreadsheet Macro Injection) vulnerability in the session export functionality where user-controlled input (like title or project name) beginning with formula characters (=, +, -, @) wasn't sanitized, potentially allowing malicious macros to execute when opened in spreadsheet software.
**Learning:** Standard CSV sanitization (`""` wrapping and double quotes) prevents syntax corruption but does not prevent formula injection. User-controlled strings must be checked for formula characters, including full-width variants and after leading whitespace.
**Prevention:** Prepend a single quote (`'`) to field values that start with formula characters (`=`, `+`, `-`, `@` or full-width equivalents) to force spreadsheet software to treat them as text, while preserving numeric formatting for raw `number` types.
